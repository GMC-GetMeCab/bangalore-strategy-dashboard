Methodology

A progress score,
not a PR counter.

The score measures completion of the Bangalore strategy’s weighted acceptance outcomes. It does not reward lines of code, commit count, or traffic volume.

01

Completion

Nine workstreams total 100 fixed points. A workstream earns partial credit only when evidence is merged, deployed, or observed. Weights do not change to make the trend look better. Historical scores are preserved when methodology changes.

02

Daily movement

Daily points equal today’s cumulative score minus yesterday’s. Relative movement is that point change divided by yesterday’s score. Negative movement is allowed when evidence regresses or a previously credited assumption is disproved.

03

Outcome data

GA4 organic sessions, GSC clicks/impressions, Beacon events and bookings remain separate. GSC uses settled dates and equal windows. Missing Beacon series are unavailable—not zero. Day-over-day movement is never called causal.

04

Sources

GA4 is observed browser traffic. Search Console is organic search visibility with a two-to-three-day lag. Atlas resolves Bangalore entities and direction. Beacon supplies bounded funnel signals. GitHub and production smoke checks provide delivery evidence.
